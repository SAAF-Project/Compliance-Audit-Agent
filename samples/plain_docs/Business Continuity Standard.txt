1. Purpose

1.1 This Standard sets the requirements for keeping critical business processes
running during a disruption and for restoring them afterwards.

2. Business impact analysis

2.1 Every business unit performs a business impact analysis each year and
determines the maximum tolerable period of disruption for its processes.

2.2 Processes with a maximum tolerable period of disruption of less than two
working days are classified as critical.

3. Continuity plans

3.1 A continuity plan exists for every critical process and names the people who
decide on its activation.

3.2 Continuity plans are tested at least once a year and the lessons learned are
recorded.

3.3 Table 1 – Recovery time objectives per critical process sets out the agreed
recovery times.

4. Crisis management

4.1 The crisis management team is chaired by the Chief Operating Officer and
meets within four hours of the declaration of a crisis.

4.2 Communication with policyholders, staff and the supervisory authority during
a crisis is coordinated by the Communications department.

5. Dependencies

5.1 Dependencies on buildings, systems, staff and suppliers are recorded for
every critical process.
