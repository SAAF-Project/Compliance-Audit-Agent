1. Mandate

1.1 The Risk Management Function assists the Board of Directors in the effective
operation of the risk management system.

1.2 The function is independent from the business units whose risks it monitors
and has direct access to the Board of Directors.

2. Responsibilities

2.1 The Risk Management Function maintains an aggregated view of the risk
profile of the Company and reports on it to the Risk Committee every quarter.

2.2 The Risk Management Function confirms the criticality assessment of proposed
outsourcing arrangements.

2.3 The Risk Management Function reviews the remediation plans for material data
deficiencies and monitors their progress.

2.4 The function advises the Board of Directors on strategic decisions such as
major projects, investments and changes to the business model.

3. Authority

3.1 The function has unrestricted access to all records, staff and premises of
the Company that it needs to carry out its responsibilities.

3.2 The Chief Risk Officer may escalate any matter directly to the chair of the
Risk Committee.

4. Review

4.1 This Charter is reviewed every two years and is approved by the Board of
Directors.
