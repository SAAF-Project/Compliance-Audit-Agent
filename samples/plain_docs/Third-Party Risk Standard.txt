Third-Party Risk Standard

Document type: Standard
Owner: Head of Procurement
Version: 2.4
Approved: 2025-09-02
Classification: synthetic sample, not a real policy

1. Purpose

1.1 This Standard describes the minimum controls for the selection, contracting
and oversight of third parties that deliver services to the Company.

1.2 It supports the Outsourcing Policy and applies in addition to it whenever a
third party performs a critical or important function.

2. Risk classification

2.1 Each third party is classified as low, medium or high risk on the basis of
the criticality of the service, the sensitivity of the data involved and the
ease with which the third party can be replaced.

2.2 The classification is recorded in the third-party inventory and is reviewed
when the scope of the service changes and at least once every two years.

3. Due diligence

3.1 A documented due diligence assessment must be completed before any contract
for a critical or important function is signed.

3.2 The assessment covers the financial soundness of the service provider, its
technical ability, its information security controls, its business continuity
arrangements and any conflict of interest.

3.3 The results of the assessment are stored in the third-party inventory
together with the evidence received from the service provider.

3.4 For medium and low risk third parties a simplified questionnaire is
sufficient.

4. Contracting

4.1 Contracts are drafted on the basis of the standard contract templates
maintained by the Legal department.

4.2 Deviations from the standard clauses on confidentiality, liability and data
protection require the written approval of the Legal department.

5. Ongoing oversight

5.1 High risk third parties are reassessed every year; medium risk third parties
are reassessed every three years.

5.2 Incidents at a third party that affect the Company are recorded in the
incident register within two working days.

5.3 The Head of Procurement reports the number of overdue reassessments to the
Risk Committee every quarter.

6. Concentration

6.1 Procurement monitors the share of critical services that is delivered by a
single third party and reports concentrations above forty percent to the Risk
Management Function.
